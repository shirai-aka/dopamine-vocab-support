ドーパミン学習 (Dopamine Learning)

A learning app for iPhone that uses flashy effects to help you keep reviewing vocabulary and grammar. You can switch between English, French, German, Spanish, Korean, classical Japanese, kanbun, biology, chemistry and physics within a single app. Correct answers fill a meter, and the more spaced out your reviews are, the bigger the reward.

ドーパミン学習 (Dopamine Learning)

Support

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This English page is a reference translation of the Japanese original, provided for convenience. The Japanese text is the official version; where the two differ, the Japanese text applies.

“ドーパミン学習” (Dopamine Learning) is an iPhone app that lets you keep practicing recalling vocabulary and grammar through multiple-choice and spelling questions, accompanied by light, sound and reel effects. You can switch between English (vocabulary and grammar), French, German, Spanish, Korean, classical Japanese (vocabulary and classical grammar), kanbun (key words and syntax), and biology, chemistry and physics (terms and practice questions) within a single app.

How to Use

  • On first launch, choose a subject, then choose the level of the new words or questions that will appear. You can switch subjects at any time with the subject button at the top left of the home screen, and the level can be changed per subject in Settings.
  • Coins, the meter, today’s study count and the study streak are shared across all subjects.
  • “学習スタート” (Start studying) presents questions in sets of 10. Words you get wrong are asked again within the same set.
  • The better you know a word, the more the question format changes (for example: English→Japanese multiple choice, Japanese→English multiple choice, typing the spelling; for kanbun, meaning→reading).
  • For each subject, the home screen shows a button for memorizing vocabulary and a button for solving questions (English grammar, classical grammar, kanbun syntax, and science practice questions). Classical Japanese also has “ゴロで覚える” (Learn by chanting), for memorizing things like auxiliary verb conjugations and connections by reciting them.
  • If you don’t know the answer, press “わからない” (I don’t know) to see the correct answer.
  • Correct answers fill the dopamine meter, and when it reaches 100% a jackpot effect always plays.
  • Coins you collect can be exchanged in the shop for visual effects (correct-answer effects, backgrounds, reel symbols).
  • The length of effects, flashing, screen shake, sound effects, read-aloud and vibration can be changed on the Settings screen.

FAQ

Do I need to register an account?

No. There is no account creation or login feature.

Where are my study records stored?

Study records, coins and settings are stored on your device. If you delete the app, the saved data is deleted as well.

Are the jackpots and reels random?

No, they are not a lottery. A jackpot always happens when the meter reaches 100%, and the meter increases only based on your correct answers. There is no feature for betting coins, and coins cannot be bought with money.

Are there ads or purchases?

A banner ad is shown at the bottom of the home screen and the results screen. No ads are shown on the question screen while you are studying. There are no in-app purchases.

I can’t hear the read-aloud

It cannot be heard when the device’s silent switch (silent mode) is on or the volume is low. Please also check that “外国語の読み上げ” (Foreign-language read-aloud) is turned on in Settings. There is no read-aloud for classical Japanese or kanbun (because historical kana orthography cannot be read correctly).

I want to stop the review reminders

Turn off “復習のお知らせ” (Review reminders) on the Settings screen. Reminders are off by default, and even when turned on they are limited to once a day.

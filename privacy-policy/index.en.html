ドーパミン学習 (Dopamine Learning)

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This English page is a reference translation of the Japanese original, provided for convenience. The Japanese text is the official version; where the two differ, the Japanese text applies.

“ドーパミン学習” (Dopamine Learning; the “App”) is a learning app for iPhone for practicing recall and reviewing vocabulary and questions in English, second foreign languages, classical Japanese, kanbun, science and other subjects, switching between subjects within a single app. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. Information the App Obtains

The App does not ask you to enter, and does not obtain, information that identifies you personally, such as your name, address, telephone number or email address.

To provide its learning features, the App stores the following information on your device.

  • Learning records for each word and question (number of correct and incorrect answers, date and time last answered, date and time of the next review, time taken to answer)
  • In-app state such as coins, the meter and items exchanged
  • Results such as today’s study count, study streak and accuracy rate
  • Settings such as effects, volume, read-aloud, vibration, reminders, the selected subject and the course for each subject

This information is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer’s server.

2. Purposes of Use

  • Deciding which words and questions to review and in what question format
  • Displaying study progress and results
  • Saving in-app state such as coins, and settings
  • Review reminders (only if you turn them on)
  • Displaying ads

3. Network Communication and Provision to Third Parties

Study records and settings are never sent to the developer’s server. Except where required by law, the App does not sell or provide user information to third parties.

However, to display ads, the App communicates with an ad delivery service (Google AdMob), and the ad service provider may obtain information necessary for delivering ads (see “4. Advertising”).

No external tools for access analytics or crash analytics are used. Foreign-language read-aloud uses the standard iOS speech synthesis built into the device.

4. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to deliver ads. Ads are shown as banner ads at the bottom of the home screen and the results screen. No ads are shown on the question screen while you are studying.

Ad requests are marked as requests for non-personalized ads, and the content rating of the ads shown is limited to the equivalent of “General” (all ages).

Ad services such as Google AdMob may obtain device IDs, product interaction (such as ad views and taps), advertising data, coarse location, crash data, performance data and other diagnostic data for purposes such as displaying ads, measuring ad effectiveness and preventing fraud.

For how Google handles information, please see the following.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. App Tracking Transparency

The App does not use information for the purpose of tracking users across other companies’ apps or websites. For that reason, it does not show the permission dialog under Apple’s App Tracking Transparency. If ad delivery is changed in the future to involve tracking, we will ask for permission beforehand and update this policy.

6. Device Permissions and Notifications

The App does not request permissions such as location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Only if you turn on “復習のお知らせ” (Review reminders) on the Settings screen will the App ask for notification permission. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device; no external server is used.

7. Data Management and Deletion

Study records and coins can be deleted with “学習記録をリセット” (Reset study records) on the Settings screen. If you delete the App, the data stored on your device is deleted as well.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information such as names or email addresses from anyone, regardless of age. Ads are limited to non-personalized ads, with content restricted to all-ages material.

9. Revisions

This policy may be revised in response to new features, the introduction of external services, or changes in laws or platform rules. Revisions will be published on this page.
